DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by Synerbit OPC Private Limited

1. Overview and Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the BusView smart school transportation management and live bus tracking platform, developed and operated by Synerbit OPC Private Limited ("we", "us", or "our"). We recognize that school transportation data involves student safety and parental trust, and we maintain stringent technical and organizational standards to safeguard this information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide live transportation monitoring:

  • Institutional Information: School name, administrative contact details, authorized staff credentials, and fleet configuration details.
  • Vehicle Telematics & Location Data: Real-time geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude), vehicle velocity, heading, and transit timestamps transmitted by drivers during active trips.
  • Route and Stop Roster Data: Configured bus route paths, designated pickup/drop-off stops, and student stop associations provided by the school.
  • Account Credentials: Secure login identifiers for school administrators, drivers, and parents.

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through BusView is used solely for operational transportation functions:

  • Displaying live bus location markers on authorized parent and school dashboard maps.
  • Calculating estimated arrival times (ETAs) for upcoming stops.
  • Providing schools with administrative operational visibility and safety accountability logs.
  • Communicating critical service updates, technical alerts, or customer support responses.

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker school, driver, or parent personal data to advertisers or commercial third parties.

4. Data Access and Security Controls

BusView enforces role-based access control. Parents can only access location data corresponding to their child's specific assigned bus during designated route operational hours. Driver location broadcasting is active only during designated route trips. All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard TLS encryption protocols.
