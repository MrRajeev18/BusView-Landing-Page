THE REALITY OF SCHOOL TRANSPORT

School Transportation Shouldn't Be a Daily Guessing Game.

Every morning and afternoon, thousands of parents and school transport coordinators deal with preventable friction, anxiety, and repetitive communication.

Parents Often Don't Know Where the School Bus Is

Parents wait by roadside stops in harsh weather—extreme sun, winter cold, or heavy rain—unsure whether the bus has already departed, is delayed by traffic, or has not left campus yet.

"Did we miss the bus by 2 minutes, or is it stuck in morning traffic? Should I drive my child to school instead?"

Schools Face Massive Coordination Challenges

School front desks and transport managers are swamped with dozens of frantic calls during morning and afternoon pick-and-drop windows, making proactive supervision virtually impossible.

"Our administrative phone lines ring continuously for 45 minutes every morning just answering 'Where is Bus 7?'"

Communication Gaps Create Unnecessary Uncertainty

Drivers cannot safely take phone calls while operating heavy vehicles in city traffic. Without a connected digital channel, minor delays turn into widespread parental panic.

"Calling the driver directly while they are driving is unsafe and creates significant distraction on the road."

COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, straightforward answers about the BusView platform, tracking workflow, and pilot participation.

BusView is a connected digital platform developed by Synerbit OPC Private Limited to make school transportation transparent, manageable, and convenient. It connects schools, parents, and drivers with live bus tracking, arrival updates, and centralized fleet monitoring.

During active morning or afternoon trips, the bus driver's device shares real-time location data securely with the BusView platform. School administrators can monitor the bus moving on an interactive dashboard map, while parents can view their child's specific assigned bus location and estimated arrival time on mobile.

BusView is designed for three stakeholders: school administrators/transport managers (who oversee fleet operations), parents/guardians (who track their child's assigned bus), and bus drivers (who broadcast location updates through a simple interface). Primary onboarding is initiated at the school level.

Schools can apply through our online enquiry form by selecting "Free Pilot Programme". Our team will coordinate with your school transport supervisor to verify routes, set up driver accounts, and guide your team through a pilot deployment.

Yes. The pilot programme is completely free of charge. There are no upfront fees, hidden charges, or mandatory commitments during the pilot phase. The purpose of the pilot is to enable schools to experience the platform firsthand and provide constructive feedback.

Getting started is simple: the school needs basic transport roster details (bus numbers, assigned drivers, and stop lists) and a standard internet-enabled device or smartphone for the driver during transit. No specialized, expensive vehicle alterations are required.

Once a school enrolls and configures student transport assignments, parents receive secure login credentials or an activation link. When logged in, parents can view their child's assigned bus live on the map during scheduled trip hours.
