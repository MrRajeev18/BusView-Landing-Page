The BusView Feature Suite

Grounded in actual product capabilities developed by Seniorbit OPC Private Limited to deliver reliable, accurate, and secure school bus tracking.

CAPABILITY A

Live School Bus Tracking

BusView continuously monitors active school buses, plotting real-time movement on a crisp, responsive map canvas.

• Map-based navigation: Follow the assigned bus across designated roads and intersections.
• Vehicle & Route Identification: Clear bus registration plate numbers, bus IDs, and route labels.
• Journey Transparency: Know if the bus has just begun, is midway, or approaching the school gates.

CAPABILITY B

Estimated Arrival Time (ETA)

Uncertainty around bus arrival causes stress and lost time. BusView computes realistic estimated arrival times based on current movement and remaining stops.

• No More Waiting at Roadside: Prepare students right on time before the bus arrives.
• Pickup & Drop-off Visibility: Reliable ETAs for both morning school drop and afternoon home return.
• Adaptive Calculation: Adjusts dynamically when traffic slows down or speeds up.
CAPABILITIES C & D

Parent Simplicity & Institutional Oversight

Intuitive Parent Experience

Designed with working parents in mind. The app opens immediately to the active bus view, eliminating unnecessary navigation steps during hurried mornings.

  • Single-screen status overview
  • Driver phone shortcut for emergencies
  • Zero complicated configurations

School Administration Dashboard

Gives school principals and transport managers complete visibility into fleet movements, route progress, and driver status in a unified operational interface.

  • Multi-bus live map tracking
  • Route assignment and driver roster
  • Historical trip timestamps for administrative accountability
CAPABILITY E

Driver Location Updates

A simple, distraction-free interface built specifically for school bus drivers. With one tap, drivers share their location with school administration and parents.

• High-contrast UI: Clearly readable in bright sunlight on dashboard phone mounts.
• No Complex Typing: Large buttons for trip start and stop departure updates.
• Optimized Battery & Data: Lightweight telemetry protocols ensure minimal battery drain.
