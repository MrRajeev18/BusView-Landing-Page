How BusView Works in Practice

Three simple, connected steps that transform daily school transportation from a stressful guessing game into an organized, transparent operation.

1
STEP 1 • INITIAL SETUP

School Coordinates Transportation Information

The school administrator or transport coordinator logs into the BusView web console to input existing transportation details:

  • Bus numbers, driver contact details, and assistant information
  • Designated bus routes and pickup/drop-off stops
  • Student stop assignments (so parents only see their child's specific bus)
Setup Time: Most schools complete configuration in less than 24 hours with support from the BusView team.
2
STEP 2 • ON THE ROAD

Driver Begins Route & Shares Location

When the morning or afternoon run commences:

  • The driver opens the high-contrast Driver App and taps "Start Trip"
  • Real-time GPS coordinates are transmitted securely every few seconds to the platform
  • The driver stays focused strictly on the road, free from distracting incoming calls
3
STEP 3 • REAL-TIME VISIBILITY

Connected Real-Time Visibility for All

Both schools and parents gain clear, synchronized information:

  • Parents see their assigned bus live on the map and receive updated arrival times
  • Schools monitor all fleet vehicles simultaneously from the command dashboard
  • Automatic trip logs record timestamps for administrative review and accountability
