About BusView

BusView is a technology initiative by Synerbit OPC Private Limited, focused on building practical digital solutions for everyday challenges in school transportation.

Our Purpose

School transportation is one of the most critical daily touchpoints between an educational institution and families. Yet for decades, it has remained largely uncoordinated, relying on stressful telephone calls, roadside guesswork, and fragmented communication.

At Synerbit OPC Private Limited, we believe technology should be practical, accessible, and grounded in real operational realities. Rather than pushing complicated hardware or theoretical features, we engineered BusView to solve the immediate daily problems:

For Schools

Providing school leadership and transport in-charges with a centralized operational view to supervise fleet activities calmly, safely, and transparently.

For Parents

Delivering peace of mind with live bus tracking and dynamic arrival times so families never have to wait in the rain or worry about delays.

Our Technology Principles

✓

No Unnecessary Complexity

Software that can be configured in minutes and used comfortably by school staff and drivers without intensive training.

✓

High Data Integrity & Student Privacy

✓
